What we ask every host before a listing goes live

Kashrus, in writing

Which rav or vaad kashered the kitchen, when it was last checked, whether meat and dairy are truly separate, and what the Pesach arrangement is. Recorded on the listing, not negotiated over WhatsApp.

Measured walks

Minutes on foot to the nearest year-round minyan and to the mikvah — walked and timed, not estimated. Eruv boundaries are drawn on the map, with the erev Shabbos status line linked.

Shabbos ready

Blech, urn, hot plates, lamps, pre-set timers, keypads in Shabbos mode, generator. Photographed at intake so you know what is waiting for you when you pull in Friday afternoon.
